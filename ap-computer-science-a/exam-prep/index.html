Z-AP Computer Science A

AP Exam Prep: 15 Full Practice Exams

Each exam follows the AP Computer Science A format used since May 2026: 42 multiple-choice questions and the four free-response question types. Every question is original. Every multiple-choice answer was checked by running the code in Java, and every free-response model solution was compiled and tested.

Why these practice exams?

These tests are made to help you prepare for the AP Computer Science A Exam. This is the place to make mistakes: make them here, learn from them, and don't repeat them on the real exam.

The score matters less than what you learn. After you submit, read every trace and every scoring point, especially for questions you got right by guessing.

For teachers

Each test has its own link to assign, for example kzogovic.github.io/zmath/ap-computer-science-a/exam-prep/test-1/. Students see scores only after they submit. Multiple choice is scored automatically with a full trace for every question; students score their own code against the scoring guide, and the page flags common errors (== with Strings, missing return, off-by-one bounds, removing inside an enhanced for loop). The results page shows a time-away report and can be printed or saved as a PDF.

There are 5 free-response problems of each type, so the 15 tests reuse them; the multiple choice is different in every test. Predicted AP score: 91–100% → 5 · 70–90% → 4 · 50–69% → 3 · 30–49% → 2 · below 30% → 1.
