Why these practice exams?

These tests are made to help you prepare for the AP Calculus BC Exam. This is the place to make mistakes: make them here, learn from them, and don't repeat them on the real exam.

The score matters less than what you learn. The most important thing is to understand the concepts and apply the skills you have learned to solve problems. After you submit, read every solution and scoring point carefully, especially for questions you got right by guessing.

For teachers

Each test has its own link to assign, for example kzogovic.github.io/zmath/ap-calculus-bc/exam-prep/test-1/. Students see their scores only after they submit. Multiple choice is scored automatically, with a worked solution for every question. Free response is self-scored against point-by-point scoring guidelines; the page compares the student's numbers with the expected values and flags blank answers. Students can print or save their results page as a PDF to turn in.

Test integrity: students must agree to the test rules; the test opens in full screen where the device allows it; copying and pasting are turned off; every time a student leaves the page (another app, tab, call or notification) is recorded with the time and length and shown at the top of the results page. Like the real exam, students cannot return to a part after it ends. For a full lockdown, use iPhone/iPad Guided Access, Android app pinning or managed school Chromebooks.
