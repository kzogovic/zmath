For teachers

Students may use an approved graphing calculator or the Desmos calculator built into Bluebook; each test page shows the formula sheet and the official approved-calculator list. Each test has its own link to assign, for example kzogovic.github.io/zmath/ap-statistics/exam-prep/test-1/. Students see their scores only after they submit. Multiple choice is scored automatically; free response is self-scored against a strict rubric, with common mistakes flagged automatically. Students can print or save their results page as a PDF to turn in.

Test integrity: students must agree to the test rules; the test opens in full screen where the device allows it; copying and pasting are turned off; every time a student leaves the page (another app, tab, call or notification) is recorded with the time and length and shown at the top of the results page. For a full lockdown, use iPhone/iPad Guided Access, Android app pinning or managed school Chromebooks.
